# Methods and interpretation

How are housing tenure and neighborhood attachment associated with insurance, displacement, and recovery burdens?

Survey-based comparisons and adjusted models. Respondent-level data and qualitative material require controlled access; causal effects are not established by the associations.

## Analytical sequence

Construct hazard-aligned insurance measures and factor scores, join derived responses, and estimate tenure and attachment associations with recovery outcomes. Separate descriptive figures, adjusted models, and exploratory mediation/measurement analyses.

## Sources and scope

Gulf Coast disaster-recovery survey and associated coded measures.
